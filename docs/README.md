# MishMash
### Your recipes. Your meals. Your plan.

**MishMash is a command-line meal prep planner for people who prepare meals in advance and prefer typing commands.** Keep your recipes in one place, plan your meals, and turn your plan into a grocery list.

**[Get started](UserGuide.md#quick-start)** · **[User Guide](UserGuide.md)** · **[Meet the team](AboutUs.md)**

## From recipe book to meal prep

| What you want to do | How MishMash helps |
| --- | --- |
| Build your recipe collection | Add recipes with ingredients and nutrition information, then view, edit, or delete them. |
| Plan ahead | Generate a meal plan from your recipes, replace meals, and adjust portions. |
| Apply your preferences | Set dietary preferences and ingredient exclusions for meal planning. |
| Review nutrition | Save daily nutrition targets and check your plan for shortfalls. |
| Prepare your shopping | Generate and view a grocery list based on your meal plan. |
| Track what you eat | Record meals and review your recorded nutrition totals. |

MishMash automatically saves your data on your computer and restores it when you launch from the same working directory.

## Start planning

You will need **Java 25** and a terminal. Follow the [Quick Start](UserGuide.md#quick-start) to obtain and launch MishMash.

1. **Add your recipes.** Enter at least three recipes with their ingredients and nutrition information.
2. **Generate a meal plan.** Use `generate-plan d/3` to plan three days, then review it with `view-plan`.
3. **Prepare your groceries.** Run `generate-list`, then `view-list` to see the ingredients you need.

For command formats and examples, see the [command summary](UserGuide.md#command-summary). If you change your meal plan, generate the grocery list again to keep it up to date.
